## Additional Steps to Rank on Google's First Page

### 🎯 High Priority Actions

#### 1. **Content Creation & Blogging**
Create a blog section with regular content targeting card game keywords:
- "Best New Card Games 2026"
- "How to Choose a Strategic Card Game"
- "Card Games vs Board Games: What's the Difference?"
- "Top 10 New Card Games for Families"
- "Card Game Strategy Tips"
- Regular blog posts (2-4 per month) will significantly boost SEO

#### 2. **Page Speed Optimization**
- Test your site speed with [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/)
- Optimize images (compress, use WebP format)
- Minimize JavaScript and CSS
- Enable browser caching
- Consider using a CDN

#### 3. **Backlinks Strategy**
Getting backlinks is crucial for ranking:
- **Press releases** about your Kickstarter launch
- **Gaming blogs** - Reach out to card game reviewers
- **Reddit** - Engage in r/boardgames, r/cardgames communities
- **Kickstarter** - Your campaign page will link to your site
- **Social media** - Share your content on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube
- **Guest posts** on gaming websites
- **Product directories** - List on board game databases

#### 4. **Google Business Profile**
- Create a Google Business Profile
- Add your website URL
- Get reviews from early backers/testers

#### 5. **Social Signals**
- Active social media presence (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube)
- Share gameplay videos, tutorials, card reveals
- Social engagement signals help with rankings

#### 6. **User Experience Signals**
- **Low bounce rate** - Ensure your content is engaging
- **High time on site** - Make content interesting
- **Low exit rate** - Clear call-to-actions
- **Mobile-friendly** - Already done, but test thoroughly

### 📊 Medium Priority Actions

#### 7. **Local SEO** (if applicable)
- If you have a physical location or local presence
- Add location-based keywords
- Create location pages if you sell locally

#### 8. **Video Content**
- Create YouTube videos about your card game
- Embed videos on your site
- YouTube videos can rank in Google search results
- Titles like "Void Count - New Card Game Review" or "How to Play Void Count Card Game"

#### 9. **Email Marketing**
- Build an email list (you already have waitlist)
- Send regular updates with links back to your site
- Include blog posts and updates

#### 10. **Competitor Analysis**
- Research what other card games rank for "card game" and "new card game"
- Analyze their content strategy
- See what backlinks they have
- Tools: Ahrefs, SEMrush, or free tools like Ubersuggest

### 🔍 Long-term Strategy

#### 11. **Regular Content Updates**
- Update your homepage content regularly
- Add new sections as your game evolves
- Keep content fresh and relevant

#### 12. **User-Generated Content**
- Encourage reviews and testimonials
- Create a community around your game
- User content adds fresh keywords naturally

#### 13. **Technical SEO Audits**
- Run monthly SEO audits
- Fix broken links
- Check for crawl errors in Google Search Console
- Monitor page speed

#### 14. **Analytics & Monitoring**
- Set up Google Analytics 4
- Set up Google Search Console
- Monitor:
  - Which keywords bring traffic
  - Which pages perform best
  - User behavior
  - Search rankings

## Important Notes

⚠️ **SEO is a long-term strategy** - Don't expect immediate results for highly competitive terms like "card game"

✅ **Focus on long-tail keywords first** - "new strategic card game 2026" is easier to rank for than "card game"

✅ **Quality over quantity** - Better to have fewer, high-quality backlinks than many spammy ones

✅ **User experience matters** - Google rewards sites that users love

✅ **Consistency is key** - Regular content updates and engagement
